# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this capstone a harassment-free, welcoming
experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, PRs, and reviews.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Credit others' work and cite sources.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the class.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Plagiarism or academic dishonesty.
- Committing another student's code without attribution.

## Academic integrity

This is a graded course. You may read and learn from any code, but you must
**write your own contribution** and be able to explain every line you submit
during review or viva. Copying a teammate's PR without understanding it is
treated as academic dishonesty.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the instructor or maintainers. Consequences range
from a warning to removal from the project, at the instructor's discretion.
